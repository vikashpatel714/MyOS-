Ek company thi. Wahan do tarah ke log kaam karte the — System log (S1, S2), jinke paas hamesha VIP pass tha, aur User log (U1, U2, U3), jo mehnat toh bahut karte the, par unke paas koi pehchaan nahi thi.

The Rule

Jab bhi koi VIP (System) office me ghusta, sab kaam turant rukh jaata — chahe User process abhi kitna hi zaroori kaam kar raha ho, use side me dhakel diya jaata.

S1 & S2

S1 aaya, chaar ghante kaam kiya, bina kisi rukawat ke — poora respect mila. S2 bhi aaya, teen ghante kaam kiya, bina wait kiye — usse bhi full respect mila.

U1 — The One Who Waited Most

Lekin U1 — jo sabse pehle office aaya tha, sabse zyada kaam lekar — uska haal dekho. Wo kaam shuru karta, ek minute baad hi S2 aa jaata aur use side kar diya jaata. Fir se line me, fir se wait, fir se turn ka intezaar.

Aakhir me U1 ka kaam 22 ghante baad complete hua — jisme se 16 ghante sirf wait karne me gaye.

U2 & U3

U2 aur U3 ka bhi yahi haal — 10 aur 12 ghante bekar wait me nikal gaye.

No Way Up

Sabse dukh ki baat? Kisi bhi User process ko kabhi promote nahi kiya gaya. Chahe wo kitni bhi der wait kare, uski priority kabhi nahi badhi. System hamesha upar, User hamesha neeche.

Isse life ka sabak kya mila

"Jab bhi koi VIP office me ghusta, sab kaam turant rukh jaata"

Life me bhi, jinke paas "priority" hoti hai — paisa, connections, resources — unke saamne baaki logo ka kaam ruk jaata hai. Ye system ka fixed rule hai, ismein emotion nahi hai, sirf priority hai.

"U1 sabse pehle aaya, sabse zyada mehnat ka kaam liya, phir bhi sabse zyada wait kiya"

Sirf "pehle aana" ya "zyada mehnat karna" kaafi nahi hota. Agar system me tumhari priority fix nahi hai, toh baar-baar rukna padega, chahe tum kitni bhi mehnat kyun na karo.

"Kisi bhi User process ko kabhi promote nahi kiya gaya"

Agar tumhare paas "promotion path" nahi hai — yani agar tum apni situation, skills, network upgrade nahi kar rahe — toh tum hamesha neeche hi rahoge, chahe kitna bhi wait karo.

System fair nahi hota, lekin agar tum smart ho toh apni khud ki priority bana sakte ho.

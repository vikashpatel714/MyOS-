Scene 01 · 13 Feb 2026

Vartika Mam ne NIET ke ek function ke baad group banaya — "Niet unofficial". Videos maangne se shuru hua ye safar. Vernit Sir ne pehle hi din bol diya, "Aap function me aaye hi nahi." Sneha Mam ne jawab diya, "Hum aapki aankhon se dekh rahe hain."

Aur group ka admin? Vikas Sir — jo baad me is poori film ke camera man bane. Kaun jaanta tha ki ye "unofficial" group aage jaake sabse official dosti ban jayega.

Scene 03 · Mar – Apr

Canteen ki chai aur ek "jodi" ka rumour

Roz ek hi message: "Chai coffee ke liye aa jao." Sneha Mam ka. Aur jo sabse pehle pahunchta — Shubham Sir. Group ke baaki log dekhte rahe: kabhi "papa ji ke saath ghumi ghumi", kabhi Gaur City mall, kabhi Sneha Mam ke ghar fruits lekar visit.

Vikas Sir ne camera chalaya aur announcement kar di: "I like this duo." Squad ki nazar me Shubham Sir aur Sneha Mam ki jodi pakki thi. Mummy-daddy ko bhi "pata hai" — ye line khud chat me aayi. Dono ne kabhi haan bola, kabhi hansi me taal diya. Rahasya abhi bhi suspense hai 🎬

"Humare mummy daddy ko pata h." — Sneha
Scene 04 · Parties ka season

Birthday, cake aur "party kab milegi?"

8 March Shubham Sir. 10 April Sneha Mam. 15 May Vartika Mam. 12–13 Aug Vikas Sir. Har birthday par cake, har cake par ek complaint: "Humne party nahi li!" Vernit Sir ne to apni khud ki party na milne ka note bhi chat me daal diya.

9 May ko Vernit Sir ne group ka naam badla — "The OG Squad 😎". Phir Omaxe, Rishikesh ka plan, Connaught Place ki movie, Jaipur ka poll. Plan Sneha Mam banati, vote hote, aur aakhir me koi na koi bolta — "Main thoda busy hoon."

Scene 05 · Gym arc

Vikas Sir ka trainer-mode

Shubham Sir ne gym join kiya, banana aur protein shake shuru. Vikas Sir turant personal trainer ban gaye aur Sneha Mam ko offer bhi kiya — fees pe negotiation chali: "Bas 150 rupya dega." Gym band hua to bhi ye teeno ro padhe. Aur hamesha ki tarah, baat ka ant hua ek hi line pe — "Khaane chalein?"

Scene 06 · 18 March & 3 April

Bimaari ka bahana, fruits ka order

Sneha Mam ghar pe "bimaar" thi. Vikas Sir ne poochha, "Aana padega kya?" Jawab aaya: "1 kg apple, banana, guava aur papaya — bas itna kaafi hoga meri recovery ke liye." Na aa paao to Blinkit se bhijwa do! Vernit Sir ne cab book kar di, aur teen log chal diye.

3 April ko Sneha Mam barish me dheere-dheere gaadi chalakar ghar pahunchi. Vernit Sir ka pehla sawaal: "Raasta to nahi bhuli?" Doosra: "Photos bhej dijiye mam, saari." Sneha Mam ne Snapchat se bhejin — squad ki gallery bharti rahi.

"Finally reached home sweet home." — Sneha
Scene 07 · 28 May

Movie plan: 11 baje cake, 12:35 pe film… aur Shubham Sir missing

Sneha Mam ki itinerary ekdum film jaisi thi — "11 am cake cutting, 11:30 movie." Phir ek-ek karke bahane aaye: Vikas Sir ki neend, Vernit Sir ka naashta, aur Shubham Sir ka college.

Sneha Mam ne solution diya: "Shubham sir, chakkar khaake gir jana. BP low bol ke aa jana." Aakhir me show 5:45 pe shift hua. Vartika Mam ne apna perfect plan dekh ke bola: "Perfect plan hai 😂".

"Every time someone is missing, what to do now?" — Sneha
Scene 08 · 10 Oct · Navratri

Khaane pe behes, aur shaanti ka samjhauta

Navratri shuru hui aur Vikas Sir ne Shubham Sir ko yaad dilaya ki veg rehna hai. Group me aadhe ghante ki behes chali: science, geography, newton ke laws, sab nikal aaye. Dono taraf se punchlines the.

Phir Sneha Mam ne referee ban ke seedhi line boli: "Food is personal choice. Khush raho sab." Behes khatam, parathe phir se agenda me.

Scene 07 · Aaj, 17 October

Phone discharge ho gaya, aur poori police chat me

Sneha Mam ka phone band, ghar wale pareshan. Group me sab pareshan — Vikas Sir ne lecture diya: "Phone off hone se pehle bata diya karo." Sneha Mam ne reply kiya: "Jab main ghar se bhaagungi to reel bana ke jaungi — Vikas ne bahut help ki thi." Vikas Sir ne haath jod liye: "Badnaami bhi meri hi reel se honi hai!"

Aur Vartika Mam ne seedhi si baat kahi: "Next time phone discharge ho to ghar pe kisi ko msg kar diya karo." Yehi hai is squad ka pyaar — daant ke saath dhyaan.

Scene 09 · July – Oct

"Try to understand the concepts rather than simply memorizing." — Vikas Sir, group ke teacher
Scene 10 · 13 October

Comedy night aur NIET ka naya image

Taruna Mam ne 4 free passes ka entry code group me daala. Sneha Mam ne dinner ka plan banaya: "No dhaba. Proper restaurant." Shubham Sir ne apni ek hi shart rakhi — "Yadav ji ke parathe 😀".

Din ke end me Shubham Sir ne apna review diya: "Pehle mujhe lagta tha NIET vahiyat jagah hai, par aaj thoda achha laga." Squad ke liye ye Oscar se kam nahi tha.
